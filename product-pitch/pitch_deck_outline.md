# Pitch Deck Outline

## Slide 1: DreamPipe

From AI Generation to AI Production.

Tagline: Make AIGC consistent, controllable, and cost-efficient.

## Slide 2: Problem

AIGC is powerful, but production teams still cannot rely on it.

- Consistency drifts across shots.
- Quality failures need manual diagnosis.
- Costs explode through uncontrolled retries.

## Slide 3: Insight

The market does not only need stronger generation models. It needs a production orchestration layer above them.

## Slide 4: Product Workflow

Creative Brief -> Creative Director -> Project Bible -> Storyboard -> Prompt Compiler -> Model Router -> AI QA -> Retry -> Final Output.

## Slide 5: Differentiators

- Consistency: shared Project Bible across all shots.
- Auto QA and Retry: diagnose, repair prompt, retry only failed shots.
- Cost-aware Routing: choose model and retry strategy under budget.

## Slide 8: Business Value

Target users:

- Marketing teams.
- Tourism and cultural promotion teams.
- Brand content teams.
- Agencies producing short-form campaigns.

Value:

- Faster creative iteration.
- Lower manual review cost.
- More repeatable brand output.
- Better budget control.

## Slide 9: Why Now

Generation models are good enough for production attempts, but the missing layer is workflow control, QA, retry, and cost management.

## Slide 10: Closing

Others generate a video. DreamPipe generates the production line.
